; The mini siege's RTS art (Engine 0.11 T9): original NES-style pixel art, one string per row,
; one character per pixel. Tiles and units are 16x16; the villagers (worker, soldier, the captain)
; and the raiders are drawn in three facings (down, up, right; the skin mirrors right for left)
; with two walking steps. The siege engines (ram, catapult) are drawn from the side, two frames
; each, whole and burnt (`-burnt-`, the skin's look for the tag `damaged`). The hall fills its
; 48x48 footprint, the watchtower 32x32, with `site-2x2` its scaffold while it goes up. Bars are
; 16x2 eighths, `offer-0`/`offer-1` the torch over an offered mission, HUD icons 8x8. Palette
; content/palettes/siege.json; frozen units are drawn with content/palettes/siege-held.json.
