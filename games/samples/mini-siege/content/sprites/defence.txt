; The mini siege's art for the walls' round (Engine 0.12 T8): original NES-style pixel art, one
; string per row, one character per pixel, in the battle's palette (content/palettes/siege.json,
; the same letters as siege.txt). The skin canvas2d-td-defence.json draws the round with it and
; reuses siege.txt's grass, road and raiders (and heart): `wall-cell` is a build cell (a stretch of
; the village's stone wall), `gate` the hall's gate the raiders run for, `camp` the road out of the
; raiders' camp (a war banner); the two towers stand on the wall, each idle and firing: `archers`
; (a villager in blue with a bow) and `ballista` (a wooden bolt thrower); the shots `shot-arrow` and
; `shot-bolt`; the cursor (`cursor-0`/`-1`, it blinks) and the HUD icon `icon-wall`. Cells, towers,
; shots and the cursor are 16x16, the icon 8x8. The skin draws the ghost tower with defence-ok or
; defence-blocked and a struck raider with defence-hit (the same letters, other colours).
