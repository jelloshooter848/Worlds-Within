; The RTS skirmish sample's art (Engine 0.9 T11): original NES-style pixel art, one string per
; row, one character per pixel. Tiles and units are 16x16, drawn in three facings (down, up, right;
; the skin mirrors right for left, and a diagonal uses its horizontal part) with two walking steps;
; buildings fill their footprint (town centre 48x48, barracks 32x32) with a scaffold frame for a
; site (and a half-built barracks, walls under the scaffold, the skin's look from progress 50);
; bars are 16x2 eighths; HUD icons 8x8; the end banners (`banner-won`, `banner-lost`, 160x48,
; Engine 0.15 T14) were made once by a throwaway script and are kept as text. Palette content/palettes/skirmish.json; the enemy
; side is drawn with content/palettes/skirmish-blue.json (the team colours swapped).
