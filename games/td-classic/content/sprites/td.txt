; The tower-defense sample's art (Engine 0.12 T5): original NES-style pixel art, one string per
; row, one character per pixel. The map's five cells (grass, the dirt road, the stone build pad,
; the cave the enemies come out of and the gatehouse they run for), the two towers (a wooden arrow
; tower and a stone cannon, each idle and firing), the two enemies (a green runner and a purple
; brute, two steps each, drawn facing right and mirrored by the skin), the two shots, the cursor
; (two frames, it blinks) and the HUD icons. Cells, towers, enemies, shots and the cursor are
; 16x16; the icons 8x8. Palette content/palettes/td.json; the skin draws the ghost tower with
; td-ok or td-blocked and a struck enemy with td-hit (the same letters, other colours).
